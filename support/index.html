Settings for grown-ups

Tap the gear at the top right of Pomi's house and answer the multiplication question. Play time, language, sound, "Pomi listens" and the privacy settings are there.

Daily play time

Choose a limit in the grown-ups' settings. When it is up, Pomi finishes what he is doing and goes to sleep. To give more time, tap the stopwatch and pass the gate.

The microphone

On iPhone and iPad, Pomi listens for a few seconds when he waits for your child to answer, on the device only. Nothing is recorded or sent. Turn it off in the grown-ups' settings or in Settings › Pomi › Microphone.

Deleting your child's data

Everything stays on your device. In the grown-ups' settings, "Delete your child's data" removes progress, drawings and finds at once. Deleting the app removes everything.
