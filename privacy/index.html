Pomi — Privacy policy

Last updated and effective: 24 October 2026

Pomi is a learning app for children aged 3–4 (younger and older children can play too), made by Wisbi VietNam, an independent developer based in Vietnam ("we"). This policy is written for parents and caregivers.

The short version. Pomi works entirely on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any information about your child or you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

What stays on your device. To work, Pomi keeps these things on the device only:

  • your child's progress and Pomi's memory (for example the books read, the English words heard, the games played and their levels, the things found for the treasure chest, the time played today);
  • your settings (sound, language, child's age, daily play time, "Pomi listens");
  • the pictures your child draws in the app.

None of this leaves the device. We cannot see it.

The microphone ("Pomi listens", iPhone and iPad). With your permission, Pomi opens the microphone for a few seconds only when he waits for your child to answer (for example after "Ready, set…"). Apple's sound classifier (SoundAnalysis), running on the device, tells Pomi only whether a voice is heard, never which words. Nothing is recorded, kept or sent anywhere. You can switch it off in the parent area or in the device's Settings (Settings › Pomi › Microphone); Pomi then plays as before. Android has no listening.

What we do not do. Pomi does not use the camera, does not record your child's voice or take photos, does not ask for your child's name, email or location, does not use advertising identifiers, does not show ads, does not link to websites or social media from your child's area, and never offers purchases to your child. No other company's code in Pomi sends data anywhere, and the app sends no crash or usage reports to us. Because we hold no data, we share none with anyone.

The parent gate. Settings, this policy and anything meant for grown-ups sit behind a parent gate (tap the gear, then answer a multiplication question).

Keeping and deleting. The information stays on the device until you delete it. In the parent area, "Delete your child's data" removes at once the progress, Pomi's memory, the treasure chest, the games played and every drawing; your settings stay. Deleting the app removes everything. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side; you can still write to us and we will answer within 30 days.

Apple and Google. The App Store and Google Play may collect information under their own policies when you download the app (for example download records or, if you allow it, crash reports sent by your device to Apple or Google). We do not receive information that identifies you or your child from them.

Children. Pomi is designed for children under 13. Because Pomi collects no personal information and sends no identifier off the device, it collects no information under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), so no parental consent for collection is needed. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent in the app before anything is collected.

Changes. If we change this policy, we will update the date above and show the new version in the app before the change applies.

Contact. Questions or complaints: Wisbi VietNam, wisbi.dev@gmail.com. If you are in the UK or the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).
